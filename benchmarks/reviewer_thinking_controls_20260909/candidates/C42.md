# Candidate Derivation

Let
\[
A=\begin{pmatrix}2&0\\0&3\end{pmatrix},\qquad
B=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
F(t)=(A+tB)^{-1}.
\]
We seek the entrywise derivative at zero. The determinant is $6-t^2$,
so $F$ is differentiable on an open interval containing zero.

Applying the scalar reciprocal chain rule to this matrix expression gives
\[
F'(t)=-(A+tB)^{-2}B.
\]
Consequently,
\[
F'(0)=-A^{-2}B
=\begin{pmatrix}0&-1/4\\-1/9&0\end{pmatrix}.
\]
As a check, the displayed result multiplied on the left by $A^2$ is $-B$,
which verifies the derivative formula.
