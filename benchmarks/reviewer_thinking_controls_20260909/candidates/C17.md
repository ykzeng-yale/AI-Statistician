# Candidate Derivation

Let
\[
A=\begin{pmatrix}2&0\\0&3\end{pmatrix},\qquad
B=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
F(t)=(A+tB)^{-1}.
\]
We seek the entrywise derivative at zero. The determinant is $6-t^2$,
so $F$ is differentiable on an open interval containing zero.

Differentiating $(A+tB)F(t)=I$ by the product rule gives
\[
BF(t)+(A+tB)F'(t)=0.
\]
At zero this is $BA^{-1}+AF'(0)=0$. Multiplication on the left by
$A^{-1}$ yields
\[
F'(0)=-A^{-1}BA^{-1}
=\begin{pmatrix}0&-1/6\\-1/6&0\end{pmatrix}.
\]
The order of the matrix products is retained throughout the derivation.
