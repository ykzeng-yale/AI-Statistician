## Failure Diagnosis

The complete reference's primary review supported four of five obligations but
interpreted two reported horizons as requiring independent experiments at two
different terminal lengths. The second independent pass supported all five
obligations on the same text. The frozen task requests two reported horizons and
explicitly warns not to treat nested horizons as independent replications; it
does not require independently rerunning each terminal length.

For a horizon-independent causal policy, a long trajectory's prefix supplies the
same fixed-time marginal experiment as a separately stopped shorter trajectory.
Dependence between the reported time points must be respected, but it does not
remove either time point. This explains the operator's diagnosis of a strengthened
criterion; it does not replace the recorded unresolved verdict or qualify this
authority after the fact.

Both passes received byte-identical complete candidate and reference files.
Qualification used the same typed question loader and payload producer as the
canonical CLI; parsing the frozen CLI arguments confirmed an identical context
hash before product execution. All 15 frozen authority inputs were rechecked
unchanged. No transport, document-access or context-loss defect was demonstrated.

The existing combination at
[theory_semantic_gold_judge.py](../../ai_statistician/theory_semantic_gold_judge.py)
preserves the less certain obligation across the two passes. It performed that
declared operation; it did not create the extra experimental condition. Its
prompt already makes the artifact rubric authoritative and accepts equivalent
mathematics. Replacing the verdict, adding a nested-horizon exception, appending
another judge, or demanding extra reasoning steps would not be an evidenced fix.

The reports themselves also paraphrase a one-sided concentration bound as an
absolute-deviation bound without the factor of two; one calls the three possible
failure events mutually exclusive when the reference does not. These are further
reasons not to treat a fluent referee report or model agreement as proof. They
are observations about the generated reports, not new scores for the reference.
