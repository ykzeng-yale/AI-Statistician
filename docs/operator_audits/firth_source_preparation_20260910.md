## Why This Resource

The next capability question should test a full research lineage after the shared
exploratory-script simplification, not another elementary referee control or a
retry of the consumed trimmed-mean task. Kosmidis and Firth's published result
offers a mathematical argument, unchanged author code, a reproducible numerical
experiment and real R execution in one source-aware setting.

The proposed core is finite Jeffreys-penalized logistic estimation under a
full-column-rank fixed design, including separation: derive the objective and
score, justify finite existence, implement a numerical method, challenge it on
fresh data, and accurately state its limitations. This is not a request for every
result in the paper, coordinatewise shrinkage, universal optimizer convergence,
unique maxima, finite-sample unbiasedness, exact Wald coverage, or novel research.
Known-source assistance is explicit; it cannot count as blind rediscovery.

No visible question, hidden gold manifest, semantic authority or product draw is
activated here. `research-l0-firth-logistic-20260910-v1` is only a reserved candidate
identifier in the source execution descriptor. Before any product call, the full
task and independent reference, negative controls, tolerances and semantic rubric
must be qualified and frozen. Mathematical scoring stays evaluator-only, outside
product code, source tools and RAG. Existing consumed tasks remain untouched.
