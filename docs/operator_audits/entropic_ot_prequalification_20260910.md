## Shared Execution Defect

Inspection before product launch found that CLI preflight called the mechanical
reference/negative suite again, even when semantic qualification was frozen.
This conflated qualification with startup verification. It was a code-path
finding; no product was launched to demonstrate it.

Startup now requires both kinds of frozen authority. Mechanical authority uses
references to the unchanged qualification record and its original input manifest.
The verifier checks hashes, task/contract identity, exact evaluator and candidate
configuration, and the recorded successful qualification. It never reruns controls
when the record is present or frozen activation is required. Missing or changed
records fail without triggering qualification. Explicit future-task qualification
remains available separately; no old result is backfilled or made eligible.

This adds no mathematical content, model prompt, repair handler, agent, scheduler,
call or acceptance-threshold change. The boolean is renamed
`require_prequalified_activation` to describe its complete meaning; no legacy
keyword fallback is added. A read-only check of this task's existing mechanical
record also passed with unchanged hashes, without creating a qualified manifest.
