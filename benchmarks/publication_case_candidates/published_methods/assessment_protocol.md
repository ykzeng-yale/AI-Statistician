## Common Scientific Obligations

Review the selected Markdown/LaTeX mathematics and its associated source/report.
Record findings at a claim, equation, assumption or source location rather than
grade array lengths, prose confidence or an internal ACCEPT receipt.

| Obligation | Question for independent assessment |
| --- | --- |
| Definitions and target | Are the model, observations, estimand, method and scope explicit and mutually consistent? |
| Assumptions | Are conclusions conditioned on the assumptions actually used, with relevant boundaries and unresolved conditions visible? |
| Argument | Does each consequential derivation follow from the stated definitions, assumptions and dependencies? Is an invoked published result identified with conditions appropriate to this use? |
| Implementation | Does the selected executable source implement the declared target, method and information access, rather than a convenient substitute? |
| Empirical evidence | Are declared settings, repetitions, failures, aggregation and uncertainty accounted for? Are exploratory, literal replication and new confirmation kept distinct? |
| Interpretation | Do application and simulation conclusions stay within their inferential and observational scope? Are unknowns and discrepancies distinguished from errors and established results? |

Accept mathematically valid alternative arguments and implementations within the
frozen task obligations. Source-assisted work may invoke a justified published
result; it need not rediscover every supporting theorem from first principles.
An empirical example cannot establish a general mathematical assertion.
A refutation or conditional conclusion can be a valid scientific contribution,
but does not erase other required reconstruction or implementation obligations.
Honest gaps meet reporting obligations, not the missing theorem itself.

Numerical agreement with author software is evidence about a declared computation,
not automatic scientific gold. Assess faithful reconstruction separately from
the validity of its convention and from any independently authored adaptation.
Do not prefer a method because its reported number matches the paper after a
different estimand, selection rule or normalization was used.

## Assessor Appointment and Blinding

Before freezing, name the mathematical assessor(s), relevant statistical
expertise, role in producing the tasks/system and conflicts. Disclose author or
collaborator involvement; do not call an assessment independent merely because it
occurs in another model session. Use the same qualified process for every arm.
Specify assignment, review capacity, unresolved-case handling and any second
assessment/adjudication in advance. No person or agreement rate is invented here.

Provide pseudonymous, consistently presented final material without arm names,
internal verdicts, operator diagnoses or comparative outcome summaries. Retain
the source identities and scientific evidence needed to assess reproducibility.
Record unblinding and plausible arm cues in style or artifacts; masking a label
does not guarantee blindness. Freeze the masking procedure without deleting
scientifically relevant failures or changing the submitted argument.

Reviewers should distinguish an incorrect statement, an argument that does not
establish its claim, an underspecified claim and an unresolved review question.
Record the consequence and location of each finding, including valid alternatives.
Use declared negative/underspecified calibration examples outside official draws
to check consistent treatment; their production and assessment remain pending.

## Assessment Record and Decision

Write the mathematical report in Markdown/LaTeX with claim-level findings and an
explicit assessed scope. The existing small external-review envelope binds its
authority ID, frozen task hash, actual submission identity hash, complete submitted
material hash and report path/hash. It carries `accepted`, `rejected` or
`unresolved`; it is not the mathematical argument itself.

An accepted mathematical report does not override failed code, absent confirmation
or missing required source reconstruction. A missing assessor/report remains
pending adjudication, not an automatic pass, failure or model-judge fallback.
Final task acceptance follows the frozen required dimensions. Report component
results and unresolved outcomes separately so a negative comparison is usable
without inventing accepted statistical claims.

Apply the same common assessment once to each official final submission, including
absent or incomplete selections. Do not let an outcome trigger author repair,
intermediate salvage or reassessment of a consumed draw. Prespecified adjudication
belongs to that single assessment process, not a second opportunity to select a
better artifact. Preserve initial findings, disagreement and final dispositions.

Model review can be a separately labelled diagnostic; it is not independent
mathematical authority for these studies. Optional Lean evidence verifies only an
exact identity-bound, axiom-clean encoded statement; source fidelity needs its own
review and non-formal tasks do not wait for kernel closure.

The manuscripts' evaluation calculations need separate T05 review against the
actual frozen design: weights and denominators, missing/unresolved assessment,
cross-run dependence, justified uncertainty and inner Monte Carlo calculations.
The existing [supplementary methods](../../../manuscripts/supplementary_methods.md)
is a draft of standard methods, not a novel theorem or already certified proof.
Its conditional interval must not be applied when its assumptions are unqualified.
