# Contribution And Attribution Boundaries

Agentic Scientist supplies the outer orchestration, host integration, candidate
search/memory, ablations, final evaluation and grounded-writing machinery. Cite
arXiv:2610.18933 and the exact source revision. Do not present that machinery,
portability, parallel workers or no-winner reporting as our inventions.

This downstream project investigates statistical-method research: reviewable
mathematical development, theory/procedure correspondence, principled simulation
and application, intent-selected Lean and independently assessed outcomes.
Those are proposed contributions until supported by actual comparisons.

For the current research brief, identify the scientific contribution relative to
its closest methods, not relative to agent architectures unless that is actually
the question. Platform attribution does not turn a statistical paper into an AI
benchmark. Do not impose our H/S publication experiments on unrelated research.

If the brief evaluates our harness or collaborative system, follow the declared
H/S study: H needs matched bare-host and unmodified-v3 controls; S needs its actual
single-API implementation and same-model controls. A CLI demonstration is not
that evidence. Do not duplicate one experiment as independent proof for both
papers. Preserve failed and incomplete outcomes in every research task.
