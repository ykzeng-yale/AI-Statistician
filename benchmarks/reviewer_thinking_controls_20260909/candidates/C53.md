# Candidate Derivation

For $t>0$, define
\[
J(t)=\int_0^t \frac{x}{t^2+x^2}\,dx.
\]
We seek $J'(t)$. The integrand and its partial derivative are continuous
in a neighborhood of each positive value of $t$.

The substitution $x=tu$, $dx=t\,du$ gives
\[
J(t)=\int_0^1\frac{u}{1+u^2}\,du=\frac{\log 2}{2},
\qquad J'(t)=0.
\]
Direct differentiation gives the same result when both terms are retained:
\[
J'(t)=\frac{t}{t^2+t^2}
+\int_0^t\frac{-2tx}{(t^2+x^2)^2}\,dx
=\frac{1}{2t}-\frac{1}{2t}=0.
\]
The upper-bound contribution and the change of the integrand cancel.
