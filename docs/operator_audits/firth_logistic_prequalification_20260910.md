## Confirmed Shared Defect

support or explicit noncoverage unless the document asserts a falsehood. That
does not prove the model followed the instruction, but it does rule out claiming
that this distinction was absent from the prompt.

The confirmed defect is loss of diagnostic information in the evaluator record.
`run_theory_semantic_gold_judge` calculated each observed status, then retained
only the hashed case identity and `correct` Boolean. A response fingerprint does
not recover a discarded classification. The operator wrapper also retained usage
and timing, not the raw responses, so the original decision cannot be recovered.

`observed_status` on short-control and long-control result rows. No new tool,
agent, repair handler, callback, scheduler, prompt, model call or task-family rule
is introduced. Expected labels, hidden documents and raw answer text remain out
of those rows. PASS/FAIL/INCONCLUSIVE combination and qualification policy are
unchanged. Existing frozen records remain opaque and are not backfilled.
